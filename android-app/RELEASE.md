**Download:** `zeppay.apk` (always the newest) → open it → allow installing from this source → Install.
Already have ZepPay 1.0.x? Install over it: your payments and settings stay. From 2.0.0 on, updates arrive inside the app.

**iPhone:** https://mananbharti.github.io/zeppay/ios/ → Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

What's new in this version is listed in `update.json` and in the app's Updates screen.

Not yet tested with a real SIM: `*99#` automation, the PIN step, UPI 123PAY on Jio. Your bank SMS is where to confirm a payment.
